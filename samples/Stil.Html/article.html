Five rules for a calm Monday

The week is decided on Friday afternoon. Five habits from teams that start Monday with a plan instead of a scramble.

ML Maja Lind 3 November 2026 5 min read

On this page

  1. Why Mondays go wrong
  2. Plan on Friday
  3. The five rules
  4. Start small

Every team we talk to has the same Monday: the first hour goes to working out who is doing what, which rooms are free and who is away. By the time the plan is clear, half the morning is gone.

Why Mondays go wrong

Monday starts with what happened since Friday: messages, a sick day, a delivery that moved. A plan made on Monday morning is a plan made under pressure, with half the facts.

We used to plan the week in the Monday meeting. Now the meeting is about the week, not about the plan.

Plan on Friday

On Friday afternoon the week behind you is fresh, and the week ahead is still calm. Twenty minutes then saves an hour on Monday.

The five rules

  1. Book the shared things first. Rooms and the van run out before people do.
  2. Put time off in the plan. A person who is away is not a free slot.
  3. Leave a fifth of each day empty. Something always comes up.
  4. One owner per task. Two owners means none.
  5. Share the plan where people look. In their calendar, not in a tool they have to open.

Start small

Pick one rule for next Friday. The ones that stick are the ones that save someone a question on Monday.
