Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Cancel from the settings, and the plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.
Does it work with our calendar app?
Tidvy shares a calendar link that Outlook, Google Calendar and Apple Calendar can subscribe to.
Where is our data stored?
