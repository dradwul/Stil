Calendar

Events, a month grid, a week time grid and an agenda. A day is tinted by --stil-day-load (0 is free, 1 is fully booked), so busy and free days show at a glance. A full planner is in calendar.html.

Events

A stil-event is a light tint of its color with a solid edge. Use a variant class, or set --stil-event-color for a color of your own. stil-event-draft marks an event that is not saved yet.

Month

A table.stil-calendar-grid with role="grid" inside a stil-calendar. Mark today with aria-current="date" and the chosen day with aria-selected="true"; stil-calendar-outside dims days of other months. Events are chips; on a narrow calendar they become stil-calendar-dots. With stil.js, the arrow keys move between the days (one day has tabindex="0"), and moving past the edge fires stilcalendaredge. Add stil-calendar-mini for a small month that only shows the tint.

Week

A time grid. On stil-week, --stil-week-days, --stil-week-first-hour and --stil-week-hours set the columns and the hours shown; --stil-calendar-hour is the height of an hour. Everything in a stil-week-col is placed by --stil-event-start and --stil-event-length in minutes from midnight. Overlapping events get --stil-event-col and --stil-event-cols and cascade, so every title stays readable. stil-week-free marks free time that can be clicked, and stil-week-now is the current time.

Right to left

Stil uses logical properties (start and end, not left and right), so dir="rtl" on <html> or any element mirrors the layout: spacing, add-ons, borders, switches and drawers. Arrow and chevron icons flip, and the arrow keys in a calendar grid follow the reading direction.

Utilities

Single-purpose classes for spacing, display, flexbox, text, borders, shadows and sizing. Responsive versions are named stil-{utility}-{sm|md|lg|xl}-{value} and apply from that breakpoint up, for example stil-d-md-none or stil-gap-lg-5.

Spacing

stil-m-* (margin) and stil-p-* (padding) take a value from 0 to 7, and margins also take auto. Add a side: t, b, s (start), e (end), x (both sides) or y (top and bottom).
